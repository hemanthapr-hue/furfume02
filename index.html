Heritage

Slow Artisanal Craft

01

Ceylon Bark Sourcing

We source only premium Ceylon cinnamon barks, harvested by hand at optimal spice density during the wet season to preserve vital essential oil complexity.

02

Cold-Press Distillation

We utilize a slow, low-temperature steam distillation system over 48 hours to extract the spice's deep warm amber heart without burning delicate sweet notes.

03

Small Batch Maturation

Each blend matures in dark glass cellars for exactly six months, letting organic wood, spice, and floral molecules molecularly fuse for absolute sillage depth.
